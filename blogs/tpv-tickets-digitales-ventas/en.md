# TPV and digital receipts: how to sell 30% more without changing TPV

**TL;DR.** A digital receipt isn't just "saving paper". It's a **second sales channel** your customer carries in their pocket. With a QR code linking to your website, a WhatsApp message and a product catalog, you can go from 2 extra sales/month to 8 or 10. The key is configuring it in **20 minutes**, not changing your TPV. If you use Tactil, ICG, Bridge or Clover, the function already exists—you just need to activate it.

## Why digital receipts sell more

When a customer pays at a traditional POS, they get a paper slip that gets lost. When they receive a digital receipt with:

- **A QR code to your website or online store.**
- **A message with your WhatsApp.**
- **A catalog of products or services.**
- **A discount code for the next purchase.**

That receipt becomes **a second point of contact with built-in sales**.

> 78% of Spanish consumers prefer digital receipts over physical ones (Nielsen, 2025). 45% keep the receipt to buy again.

## How to set it up in 20 minutes (any TPV)

### Step 1: Activate digital receipts

In **Tactil**, **ICG**, **Bridge** or **Clover**, find:
- `Settings` → `Receipts` → `Digital` or `Email/SMS receipt`.
- Check: "Include website QR", "Include WhatsApp".

You don't need a technician. It's just a checkbox.

### Step 2: Add the QR to your website or store

Your website needs:
- A featured product or monthly offer.
- A "Buy now" button leading to cart or form.
- The QR points directly to that section.

**Quick tool:** generate the QR at [qrcode-monkey.com](https://qrcode-monkey.com) or [goqr.me](https://goqr.me) and link it from your site or TPV.

### Step 3: Include WhatsApp with a pre-written message

Instead of a standalone number, show:
```
Lost something? Message us 👉 hello@youremail.com
```
Or with WhatsApp:
```
Thanks for your purchase! Want to order again? Write "Orders" to WhatsApp and we'll serve you right away.
```

This **doubles the repeat rate** in businesses that try it.

### Step 4: Add a discount code

10% off the next purchase, valid 15 days. Customers save it and come back.

### Step 5: Measure

- How many scan the code?
- How many message WhatsApp?
- How many use the discount?

In 2 weeks you know if it works.

## The error that kills digital receipts

**Making it "just to be modern".** A digital receipt without a QR, WhatsApp or offer is just... another digital receipt. It sells nothing.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

**Does every TPV have digital receipts?**
Most do. In Tactil, ICG and Bridge it's included. In Clover there are modules. If your TPV is old and doesn't have it, ask for an update.

**Is it expensive?**
No. Digital receipts are an included feature. What costs is having a website or WhatsApp to point to.

**Do customers prefer paper?**
Less than you think. Among younger businesses, 80% prefer digital. Among over 55s, 40%. Offer both the first week: "Do you prefer paper or digital?".

**What if my website doesn't sell?**
Then point to Instagram, to a WhatsApp, to a PDF catalog. You don't need an online store.

**How long does the discount last?**
15-30 days is enough. If it's unlimited, the customer never uses it.

**What if I don't have a website?**
Make a simple landing page with your top 5 products and a WhatsApp. Costs 0€ with Canva or Linktree.
