# If I own a bar, what can I automate? 5 processes that give you back hours

**TL;DR.** Don't try to automate everything. Automate what steals your time from behind the counter: digital receipts, reservations, inventory, suppliers, and payroll. Each gives you back 2-3 hours/week without losing the personal touch of your bar. The first audit of these 5 points costs **0 €**.

## The underlying problem

You opened your bar full of dreams. But every morning you wake up at 7, open, serve, close... and have no time to **think about the business**. That's the trap of bars: the owner works *in* the bar instead of *running* the bar.

Automation isn't about replacing you. It's about removing you from tasks that don't need your judgment as owner: paper billing, writing stock on a notepad, managing suppliers via WhatsApp.

> A bar in San Sebastián that automated billing saved 4 hours/week of accounting. The owner is back on the terrace on Fridays.

## 1. POS and digital receipts (you save 100% of paper)

Your POS already generates digital tickets (Tactil, ICG, Bridge, Clover). Enable:

- **QR code to your website or menu online.**
- **WhatsApp with a pre-set message** ("Missed something? Message us 👉 hello@youremail.com").
- **Discount code** for the next visit.

Digital doesn't sell "more" on its own. It sells because the customer has your contact when the craving for a tapa hits. As we saw in [*POS and digital tickets: how to sell 30% more*](https://adimenai.com/blog/tpv-tickets-digitales-ventas), bars and shops boost 12-15% repeat visits.

**Setup:** 15 minutes. Don't change POS.

## 2. Reservations online (without losing the terrace chaos)

You use Instagram, but do you take reservations? A link in your bio or a QR on the bar with **ReservaYa** or **TheFork** lets you see the full terrace in real time and confirm tables without being glued to your phone.

**Basque Country tip:** In summer, open reservations only **before 8:30 pm**. Older customers order without reservation; younger ones book. You avoid the one-hour rush.

## 3. Inventory and suppliers (ditch the aluminum notebook)

The smaller the business, the more you forget to reorder. Inventory isn't just a "big business" problem.

- **Inventory app** (Sortly or a shared spreadsheet).
- **Suppliers with auto-orders:** If you use Coren or Avinea, connect their API to alert you when stocks drop below threshold.

> Real case: Bar Txepetxa (Getxo) cut beer keg shortages from **2% to 0.1%** using a weekly 30-minute inventory check.

## 4. Payroll and HR (without leaving the bar)

You hire waiters, but payroll takes 8 hours/month.

- **Accounting firm with AI** that calculates overtime automatically.
- **Shared calendar** (Google Calendar) with shifts and holidays.

You don't need an HR department. You need 2 tools that don't fail you on Mondays.

## 5. Messaging and deliveries (your WhatsApp is your corner shop)

Do customers order to go? Your WhatsApp is already a store. Just set up:

- Pre-set message template: "Want the same? Type 'Order' and I'll get it ready."
- "Reorder last order" button for regular customers.

You don't need Deliveroo. You need a well-configured WhatsApp.

## The most common mistake (and how to avoid it)

You want to automate **everything** at once. A bar isn't a factory. Prioritize:

1. **Digital receipts** (15 min, 10 €/month).
2. **Online reservations** (30 min, free with Google Forms).
3. **Inventory** (5 min/day on app).
4. **WhatsApp with history** (nothing gets lost).
5. **Accountant** (let payroll not give you winter headaches).

## In summary

| Task | Before | Automated |
|---|---|---|
| Billing | 4 h/week | 30 min |
| Reservations | Phone glued | App |
| Inventory | Notebook | Inventory app |
| Payroll | 8 h/month | Accountant |
| Orders | Call | Pre-set WhatsApp |

Automating isn't about being cold. It's about **gaining time for what matters**: your bar, your customers, your life.
