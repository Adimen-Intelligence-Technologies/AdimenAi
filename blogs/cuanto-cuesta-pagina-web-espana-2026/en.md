# How Much Does a Professional Website Cost in Spain in 2026

**TL;DR.** In 2026, the price of a professional website in Spain ranges from **€600 to €15,000**, depending on the type: a basic corporate site costs between €600 and €2,500, an online store between €2,500 and €8,000, and a custom project (multilingual, integrations, complex logic) between €8,000 and €15,000 or more. The range is huge because it depends on three things: who builds it (freelancer, agency, platform), whether you use a template (WordPress, Shopify) or a custom build, and what the price actually includes (design, SEO, copywriting, maintenance).

## Real price ranges in 2026

These are the prices in the Spanish market this year, based on quotes from local agencies and platform comparisons:

| Website type | Freelancer | Small agency | Mid-sized agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate website (5–8 sections) | €600–1,500 | €1,500–3,000 | €3,000–6,000 |
| Website with blog and initial SEO | €900–2,000 | €2,000–4,000 | €4,000–8,000 |
| Online store (Shopify / WooCommerce) | €1,500–3,500 | €3,000–6,000 | €6,000–12,000 |
| Custom eCommerce (Next.js, headless) | — | €6,000–12,000 | €12,000–25,000 |
| Multilingual website with integrations | — | €5,000–10,000 | €10,000–20,000 |

> **Key data point:** according to ONTSI (Spain's National Observatory of Technology), 78% of Spanish SMEs with their own website renewed or replaced it in the last 3 years. The main reason was that the old one "didn't work" — it didn't sell, didn't show up on Google, or simply fell short.

## What's included (and what's NOT) at each price point?

This is where most of the surprises hide. A low price usually hides what's **not** included:

### Basic package (€600–1,500)
- ✅ Premium template (Kadence, Astra, GeneratePress…)
- ✅ Design adapted to your brand
- ✅ 5–8 sections and a contact form
- ❌ Doesn't include copywriting (you write the text yourself)
- ❌ Doesn't include technical SEO or initial rankings work
- ❌ Doesn't include maintenance (later changes are billed separately)

### Mid-tier package (€1,500–4,000)
- ✅ Custom design or a well-adapted template
- ✅ Basic copywriting (home, services, about us)
- ✅ Initial on-page SEO (tags, sitemap, Schema.org)
- ✅ Speed optimization (Core Web Vitals)
- ⚠️ Maintenance: 1–3 months free, then €40–120/month

### Online store (€3,000–8,000)
- ✅ Product catalog and payment gateway
- ✅ Shipping and invoicing setup
- ✅ Integration with your ERP or stock system
- ✅ Intuitive management panel
- ⚠️ Monthly platform cost: Shopify €39–399/month, WooCommerce + hosting €20–80/month

### Custom build (from €8,000)
- ✅ UI/UX design with prototyping
- ✅ Custom frontend and backend (Next.js, React, etc.)
- ✅ Integrations with CRM, ERP, external APIs
- ✅ Real multilingual support (not just Google Translate)
- ⚠️ Timeline: 2–4 months
- ⚠️ Requires professional maintenance (€200–600/month)

## What should you decide before requesting quotes?

Before talking to anyone, get clear on these 5 questions. It'll save you from paying for things you don't need, or discovering you're missing something halfway through the project:

1. **What do I want the website to do?** (inform, capture leads, sell online, automate)
2. **How many languages?** (Spanish only, Spanish + English, Spanish + Basque, etc.)
3. **Will I update it myself, or will someone external do it?**
4. **Do I already have a logo, photos and copy, or do they need to be created?**
5. **Where is my audience: Google, social media, local market?**

The answers determine whether you need basic WordPress, Shopify, or a custom build. If you're unsure, a **free 30-minute initial consultation** can save you months and thousands of euros.

## Common mistakes that inflate the price

- **Asking for something "that scales"** without knowing to what extent. If you sell 50 products a month, you don't need a €20,000 headless commerce build.
- **Not having your content ready.** Writing company copy costs an extra €300–1,500, depending on the number of pages.
- **Choosing a trendy platform.** Webflow, Framer, Squarespace… if your team can't manage them, you'll always depend on the provider.
- **Forgetting SEO.** A beautiful website that doesn't show up on Google is an expense, not an investment.
- **Not budgeting for maintenance.** A website needs security updates, plugin updates and backups. Realistic budget: €40–150/month minimum.

## What should a good proposal include?

A professional proposal should tell you, at minimum:

- A fixed price, or a price broken down by phase
- What's delivered (pages, sections, integrations)
- Which platform is used and why
- Who handles design, copywriting and SEO
- Delivery timeline
- What happens after delivery (maintenance, support, training)
- Whether there's an invoice with VAT (mandatory in Spain)

If a proposal just says "professional website, €800" with no breakdown, be wary.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a cheap website cost in Spain?**
Between €300 and €600 on a self-designed template (Wix, Squarespace). The problem is you won't have real SEO, it won't scale, and you'll always be dependent on the platform.

**How much does a web design agency charge in Spain?**
A small agency charges between €1,500 and €6,000 for a corporate site, and between €4,000 and €15,000 for an online store, as shown in the table above.

**How much does an online store cost?**
Between €2,500 and €12,000 in most cases. Shopify with a template and few products: €2,500–4,000. WooCommerce with customizations: €3,500–7,000. Custom headless commerce: from €10,000.

**Is the website delivered within a week?**
A basic website, yes (if you have all the content ready). An online store, 3 to 6 weeks. A custom project, 2–4 months.

**Does it include Google rankings?**
Not at the base price. Initial SEO (tags, sitemap, content, Schema.org) usually costs an extra €400–1,500. Ongoing monthly SEO, €300–1,200/month.

**Can I pay in installments?**
Yes. The usual split is 30% on signing, 40% at the midpoint, and 30% on delivery. Some agencies offer 6–12 month financing.

**What about maintenance?**
A website needs maintenance: security updates, plugins, backups, support. Realistic cost: €40–120/month on WordPress, €60–200/month on WooCommerce, €200–600/month for custom projects.
