## What does an SEO agency actually do?

Before talking about prices, it's worth separating out what many agencies sell as "SEO":

- **Technical SEO:** making sure Google can crawl and index your site properly — speed, structured data, sitemap, HTTPS, canonical tags.
- **On-page SEO:** optimizing each page individually (title tags, meta descriptions, headings, content, images).
- **Content SEO:** writing or rewriting articles, product pages and FAQs built to rank.
- **Local SEO:** showing up on Google Maps and in "near me" searches with a well-run Google Business Profile, consistent NAP (name, address, phone) and reviews.
- **Link building:** getting other relevant sites to link back to yours.
- **Analytics and reporting:** measuring with Search Console, GA4, Looker Studio and monthly reports.

**A professional agency should cover all six.** If all they offer is "publishing articles", that's a content agency, not an SEO agency.

## Questions to ask an SEO agency before signing

1. **What results should I expect at 3, 6 and 12 months?**
   - 3 months: technical and on-page SEO applied, first improvements on 20–30 keywords.
   - 6 months: ranking improvements, first leads.
   - 12 months: stable top 3–10 rankings for target keywords.

2. **Who writes the content?**
   - It should be a writer with judgment, not just unreviewed AI output.

3. **How is success measured?**
   - Organic traffic, rankings, attributable leads/sales — not just "visits".

4. **Do you use AI in your SEO work?**
   - It's fine if they do, as long as there's human oversight. AI without editorial judgment produces mediocre content.

5. **Will you do link building?**
   - If not, SEO progress will be slow. Ask exactly how and where they source links.

6. **Can I see a demo of the client dashboard?**
   - A serious agency has a proper client-facing reporting panel.

7. **What happens if targets aren't met?**
   - The best agencies have a strategy-review clause at 3–6 months.
